## TECH TREE PLANNING SECTION:
Since Saahil has made a tech tree system and I do not want it to go to waste, here are some ideas for technologies:
The player starts with basic habitats (including Electrolysis and water reclamation) (see For All Mankind S1 Jamestown), small mining vehicles (to get ice and regolith) and Vacuum Pyrolysis plants for regolith processing (really cool stuff look it up)
All early game PreFabs have built in solar panels and sufficient batteries

### Theory Technologies:
    these are expensive to research prerequisites to various practical techs, act as barriers between steps of progression
    I only want there to be one layer of these for simplicity and to not get boring.

* Advanced Fuel Production
* Fission Miniaturisation
* microwave energy beaming
* Extrabiological organics synthesis (photosynthesis without plants)
* Electro-Static Shielding
* Swarm control AI
* Advanced Ion thrusters
* Fusion
* Universal Superconductor
* Quantum Communications
* MegaStructures
* Large scale Antimatter procurement

### Early game unlocks (focus on Moon ISRU):
    These are the base of everything, so there's a lot
* Calcium Furnaces
* Extra-terrestrial electronics production
* Hydroponics
* Factories for production of building components
* Advanced ground Vehicles (Autonomous)
* Mechanical Labourers (robots for mining and construction)
* Orbital vehicle assembly
* Inter-planetary astronautical Vessels (IPAVs)

### Mid Game Unlocks (Mars colonisation):
* Advanced Landers
* Atmo processing
* Advanced Extra-terrestrial habitation (eg domes)

### Late game unlocks (beyond The Belt):
* O'Neil Cylinders
* asteroid mining
* ISVs

### Very Late Game unlocks (James Cameron's Avatar type shit)
* Dyson Sphere/swarm (no more mercury)
* Antimatter Propulsion
* TerraForming, maybe

## Not to be added:

* Techs that serve only as prerequisites for theory techs
* anything to do with maintenance (BOOORING)
* efficiency upgrades (I want all tech to matter)
